Cool consequences of hot asset reloading

Say we're testing a new art style in Chessamphetamine.  Let's let this auto-battling match play out and see what happens.

Looks like Checkmate.  Let's rewind and find a good pause spot.

Now all we do is a full git checkout of a previous commit with a different art style.  Boom, everything updates.  Check out the head again, and boom, back to the new art style.

We can even do it while it plays live.p
